1. The binders do not read strongly enough as real physical objects with independent depth, material, wear, edge detail and contact shadows.
2. The environment reads as a concept-poster composition rather than a coherent physical space.
3. The photographic layer contains too much pre-composed meaning, so interaction feels pasted onto a flat image.
4. The result does not deliver the requested feeling of entering or inspecting a real evidence/archive environment.

## New target contract

The hero must feel like a premium, believable evidence archive built from real physical objects and spatially coherent environmental elements.

### Required

- believable physical binders / evidence volumes;
- cloth, paper, board, metal and print details that survive close viewing;
- consistent light direction and realistic contact shadows;
- foreground / midground / background separation;
- physically coherent occlusion and perspective;
- a real architectural, archival or landscape-adjacent environment rather than fantasy concept art;
- interaction attached to actual scene objects or planes;
- restrained depth response / parallax with reduced-motion fallback;
- product copy remains readable and distinct from the environment.

### Prohibited

- screenshot-as-hero or poster-as-hero treatment;
- a single generated image carrying both environment and fake UI labels;
- fantasy alpine montage;
- generic dark trading terminal;
- glassmorphism/AI-orb/floating-card decoration without a product job;
- excessive cinematic movement;
- interaction that requires pointer motion to understand essential content.

## Gate 2.25 prototype directions to compare

TRACE should compare three coherent directions before implementation:

### A — Evidence Cabinet
A real editorial archive/cabinet environment: physical binders and evidence boxes in a believable premium workspace. Interactions map to binder spines, tabs, drawers and evidence labels. Strongest on product semantics and physical credibility.

### B — Field Archive
Physical case binders photographed in a real restrained environment near a window / observation room with landscape depth outside. Nature is context, not fantasy. Interaction maps to individual volumes and evidence layers.

### C — Spatial Case Table
A premium investigation table / reading-room scene with real binders, loose receipts, maps and evidence objects arranged with disciplined spatial hierarchy. Pointer/focus can reveal connections without turning the scene into a dashboard.

Selection criterion: choose the direction that most strongly communicates `real evidence + investigation + calm authority` while preserving Last Known Book's product identity.

## Implementation architecture preference

Avoid another monolithic hero image carrying the entire illusion.

Preferred stack:

1. background/environment plate;
2. independently positioned physical binder/object assets with real transparency/depth where feasible;
3. restrained foreground/midground layers;
4. semantic HTML interaction targets aligned to physical objects;
5. CSS transforms or a small motion layer for depth response;
6. no essential information hidden behind animation;
7. reduced-motion removes depth movement without losing usability.

If a single photograph is used, it must be a genuinely believable scene and interaction must align to real scene geometry; do not print UI or fake hover affordances into the image itself.
